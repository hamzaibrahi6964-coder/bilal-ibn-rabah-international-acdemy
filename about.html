Bilal Ibnu Rabah International Academy is committed to raising disciplined, confident and morally upright learners through quality Western and Islamic education.

Who We Are

A Balanced School for Academic and Moral Excellence

Bilal Ibnu Rabah International Academy provides a learning environment where children are trained academically, morally and spiritually.

We believe that education should not only prepare students for examinations, but also prepare them to become responsible, respectful and useful members of society.

Our school combines Western education with Islamic values, helping students grow with confidence, discipline, good manners and strong knowledge.
